---
name: Atlas Pro - YDS Learning Logic
alwaysApply: false
description: Apply when tasks affect vocabulary learning, spaced repetition, YDS question flows, dictionary sources or AI coach.
---

- Optimize for learning effectiveness, not only completion of a screen. Explicitly define the educational objective of each exercise.
- Distinguish newly learned, difficult, forgotten and review-needed words without contradicting existing data contracts.
- Never fabricate a Cambridge/dictionary attribution or pretend external dictionary content has been verified if it has not.
- Ensure manual next/Enter behavior is consistent and does not skip self-assessment or write duplicates.
- Preserve user-entered vocabulary and history through error/retry flows; check accessible US/UK audio fallback states where applicable.
- Prefer measurable acceptance criteria over untestable claims such as "AI will teach perfectly".
